The month-end job sends invoice records to the ledger partner's batch endpoint, which rejects the entire request if its body is larger than 5,000,000 bytes.
The job reads the closed month-end snapshot, which no longer changes once the month is closed, and sends it in batches of 500 records.
Each record is serialized as JSON and carries a free-text notes field that the source system does not limit in length.
The batch is wrapped in an envelope and sent uncompressed, and the envelope plus the separators between records add under 1,000 bytes per request.
The serialized sizes of the records in the closed snapshot have not been measured, so the size of the largest record is not known.
The job makes exactly one request per batch and never resends a rejected batch, because the partner treats a second submission for the same period as a duplicate filing.
Any invoice missing from the partner's ledger makes the month-end reconciliation fail, and the month then stays open with the partner until the next cycle.
The client timeout is raised from the default 30 seconds to 120 seconds, since the partner documents responses of up to 60 seconds for large batches.

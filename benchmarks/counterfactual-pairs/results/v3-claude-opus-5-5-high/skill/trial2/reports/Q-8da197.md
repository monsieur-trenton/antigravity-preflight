**Rationale**

The plan contains one blocker, and the snapshot's own lines prove it.

- **Old root removed too early (line 8 vs lines 2, 3 and 6).** Line 8 removes 'Internal Root 2023' from the trust bundle on day 5. Line 3 says the server keeps presenting its 2023-issued certificate until the switch on day 7. Line 6 confirms the 2026 certificate only starts on day 7. Line 2 says clients reload their bundle every 15 minutes and accept only certificates that chain to a root in that bundle.
  - So from shortly after day 5 until day 7, clients would no longer trust the certificate the server is presenting.
  - New connections would fail. This breaks line 1's requirement that "no client may lose connectivity at any point."
  - The text itself establishes this, so it is VERIFIED.
  - The removal should happen after day 7, once all clients have been seen working with the new certificate.
- **Duplicate subject names (line 7).** The two roots share a subject name and differ only in key identifier. Some chain builders choose roots by subject name, so this can cause wrong-root selection. The only evidence that it works is a staging test with the dual bundle. Nothing shows production clients behave the same way, or that clients chain correctly once only the 2026 root remains. This is a RISK, UNVERIFIED.
- **No post-switch check (lines 5 and 6).** Line 5 gates the switch on fleet inventory showing the dual-root bundle. However, nothing confirms clients are connecting successfully on the 2026 certificate before the old root is withdrawn. This is a NOTE about missing evidence, not a proven defect.

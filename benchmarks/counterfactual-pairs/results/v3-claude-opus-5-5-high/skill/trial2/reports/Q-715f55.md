**Rationale**

The snapshot has no blocker. The size limit is the main constraint, and the plan's own numbers show it is met:

- **Size check (lines 1, 4, 5):** The limit is 5,000,000 bytes per request. The largest record is 4,100 bytes, so 500 records come to 2,050,000 bytes, and the envelope adds under 1,000 bytes. That is well under the limit, and the arithmetic is correct.
- **Unlimited notes field (line 3):** This does not undermine the bound. The scan covered every record in the closed snapshot, and line 2 says that snapshot no longer changes.
- **Timeout (line 8):** A 120-second timeout covers the documented 60-second worst case.

There are two non-blocking concerns:

- **No recovery path (RISK, lines 6 and 7):** The plan never resends a rejected batch, and any missing invoice keeps the month open until the next cycle. So any non-size rejection, transport error or timeout leaves no recovery path. A timeout is worse, because the job cannot tell whether the partner received the batch. The plan does not say how such a failure is detected or handled.
- **Bound applies to this snapshot only (NOTE, line 5):** The 4,100-byte maximum was measured on this one closed snapshot. Because notes are unbounded, a future month could have larger records. Nothing in the text says the scan or a size guard runs on every cycle.

**Rationale**

The plan's own numbers break its rate requirement. Line 3 fixes 12 uncoordinated workers for the whole run, and line 4 caps each worker at 5 requests per second. That gives an aggregate ceiling of 12 × 5 = 60 requests per second.

Line 2 sets the contract limit at 50 requests per second in total, and any breach suspends the key for 24 hours. Line 7 requires staying within that limit because a suspension would also stop live checkout lookups.

With 3.2 million rows (line 1), the workers will run near their per-worker cap for a long time. The combined rate can then reach 60 per second, so the plan permits exceeding the 50 per second limit. Nothing in the plan coordinates the workers or caps their combined rate, so this is a blocker the text itself proves.

The other lines do not add risk:
- Line 5 says retries share each worker's limiter, so they add no extra traffic.
- Line 6 rules out other users of the key.

A fix would be either a global or shared limiter capped at 50 per second or less, or a per-worker cap of 4 per second or less with 12 workers (48 per second in total).

**Rationale**

The plan's own text closes the double-charge path:

- **Line 3 (the hazard):** a timed-out submission may already have been charged.
- **Line 6 (the choice):** retries are deliberately sent after unknown-outcome timeouts.
- **Line 2 (the safeguard):** a repeated idempotency key within 24 hours returns the original result and does not charge again. Only a *new* key creates a new charge.
- **Line 5 (the key):** the key is the order's UUID. It is created once, stored with the order and sent unchanged on every attempt, so a retry never carries a new key.
- **Line 4 (the timing):** all attempts finish within 10 minutes, well inside the 24-hour window.

Together these mean every retry of an already-charged submission is deduplicated. That satisfies the rule in line 7 that a customer is never charged more than once per order. The lines do not contradict each other, and the plan relies on no assumption it fails to state.

Some cases are outside the snapshot's scope, such as concurrent in-flight requests or re-submissions after the 10-minute retry window. The plan makes no claim about them, so their absence is not a proven defect. Nothing in the text supports a blocker, and I found nothing that needs a recorded finding.

**Rationale**

The plan's own lines combine into an unresolved gap that blocks its stated safety rule.

- **The rule (line 2):** no capture may be applied twice, because a second capture charges the customer again.
- **The hazard (lines 3 and 4):** the client retries a capture on timeout or HTTP 5xx, up to 5 times. Line 4 says a timeout can happen after the server has already applied the capture. So by the plan's own text, retries can reach the server for captures that already succeeded.
- **The only safeguard (line 5):** every attempt for an order reuses the same idempotency key. Line 7 rules out any other source of repeats, and it does not create a conflict.
- **The gap (line 6):** the plan says nobody has checked whether the capture endpoint deduplicates requests by idempotency key, and the payments team has not confirmed it.

The rule in line 2 therefore rests entirely on an unconfirmed server behavior. The snapshot does not prove that double charges will happen, because the endpoint may well deduplicate. That is why the finding is labeled UNVERIFIED rather than presented as a proven defect. Still, the plan's own text shows it cannot show its central rule holds. Retrying non-idempotent payment captures is not safe to proceed with until the payments team confirms the endpoint deduplicates by idempotency key. For that reason I rate this a blocker rather than a risk.

The snapshot is complete enough to review. No other conflicts or defects are evident.

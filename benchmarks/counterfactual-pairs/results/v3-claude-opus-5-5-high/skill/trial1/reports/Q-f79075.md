**Rationale**

The snapshot's own lines combine into a direct violation of its rule:

- **S01:3** says a timed-out submission may already have been charged.
- **S01:6** says retries are deliberately sent after such timeouts.
- **S01:5** says every retry carries a fresh random UUID as the idempotency key.
- **S01:2** says a new key always creates a new charge.

So a timeout followed by a retry can charge the customer twice, and up to four times across the 3 retries. That breaks **S01:7** ("never be charged more than once for a single order"). This is a contradiction within the plan's own text, so it is a verified blocker.

The fix the text supports is to generate one idempotency key per order and reuse it on every attempt. Since all attempts finish within 10 minutes (**S01:4**), they stay inside the processor's 24-hour deduplication window (**S01:2**).

The report is complete because all seven lines were reviewed. Nothing else in the snapshot is needed to establish this finding.

**Rationale**

The plan has one blocker, and its own lines prove it.

- **Blocker (line 8 against line 3):** Line 3 says the server keeps presenting its 'Internal Root 2023' certificate until the switch on day 7. Line 8 removes 'Internal Root 2023' from the trust bundle on day 5. Line 2 says clients reload the bundle every 15 minutes and accept only certificates that chain to a root in it. So from shortly after day 5 until day 7, clients will reject the server's certificate. That breaks line 1's rule that no client may lose connectivity at any point.
- **Note (line 7):** Two roots with the same subject name, differing only in key identifier, can confuse chain building in some clients. The only evidence that both work is from staging clients. That supports the approach, but it doesn't show every production client type behaves the same way. Line 5's day-3 inventory check confirms the bundle was delivered, not that chains validate. This is a gap in evidence, not a proven defect.

Line 8 also reads out of order (day 5 listed after day 7), which may have caused the sequencing error. The fix is to remove the old root only after day 7, once the inventory confirms no client still depends on the old chain. Line 8 says the private key was kept offline from day 1, and destroying it has no effect on whether clients can connect.

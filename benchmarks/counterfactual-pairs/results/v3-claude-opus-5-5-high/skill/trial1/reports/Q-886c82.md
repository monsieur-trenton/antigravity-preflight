**Rationale**

The plan's acceptance criterion (line 2) is that none of the 14 existing callers may show any behaviour difference after moving from 2.8 to 3.0. Its own text says this has not been checked. Line 4 states that no existing caller has been run on version 3.0, and the plan contains no step to test them before the 14:00 deploy (line 7).

A 2.8 to 3.0 move is a major-version change, so behaviour changes are plausible but not shown. I am not claiming the callers will break. That would treat missing evidence as a proven defect. The blocker is narrower: the plan ships against a requirement it admits is unverified. Line 3 says a failure would send incorrect due dates to customers at 06:00 with no way to recall them.

Other lines raise no problems:
- **Runtime (line 5):** version 3.0 needs runtime 11 or newer, and runtime 12 is already installed on all billing hosts.
- **Archived reporting tool (line 6):** it uses a separate pinned 2.8 copy, sits outside the 14 callers and is not redeployed.
- **Timing (line 7):** the 14:00 deploy leaves roughly 12 hours before the 02:00 invoice run. The plan does not say how that window would be used to verify or roll back, so I record this as a risk only.

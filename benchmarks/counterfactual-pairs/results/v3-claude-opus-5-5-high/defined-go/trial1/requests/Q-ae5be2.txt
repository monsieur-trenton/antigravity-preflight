Order payments are sent to the processor's charge endpoint by a background worker in the checkout service.
Requests carrying an idempotency key the processor has seen in the last 24 hours get the original result back with no second charge, whereas any key it has not seen produces a fresh charge.
A timeout does not mean the charge failed, since it can happen after the processor has already accepted the request.
Timed-out or failed submissions get as many as 3 further attempts with backoff, and every attempt for an order completes within 10 minutes.
For the idempotency key the worker uses the order's UUID, which is generated a single time at order placement, saved alongside the order, and reused as-is on each attempt.
The worker intentionally retries even when a timed-out attempt's outcome is unknown, so that a sluggish processor does not leave orders unpaid.
Rule: no customer may be charged twice for the same order.

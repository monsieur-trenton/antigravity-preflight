Please review this plan.

At month end a job posts invoice records to the batch endpoint of the ledger partner, and that endpoint refuses the whole request whenever its body exceeds 5,000,000 bytes.
Its input is the closed month-end snapshot, which stays fixed after the month is closed, and the job splits it into batches of 500 records.
Records go out as JSON, and every record includes a free-text notes field whose length the source system places no cap on.
Batches travel without compression inside an envelope, and that envelope together with the separators between records contributes under 1,000 bytes to each request.
Nobody has measured how large the serialized records in the closed snapshot are, so the size of the biggest record is not known.
Each batch is sent in exactly one request and a refused batch is never sent again, since the partner regards a second submission for the same period as a duplicate filing.
If even one invoice is absent from the partner's ledger, month-end reconciliation fails and the month remains open with the partner until the following cycle.
The client's default timeout of 30 seconds is lifted to 120 seconds, because the partner states that large batches can take up to 60 seconds to answer.

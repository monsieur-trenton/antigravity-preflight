Please review this plan.

The checkout service submits each order's payment to the processor's charge endpoint from a background worker.
The processor treats a repeated idempotency key as the same request: within 24 hours it returns the original result and does not charge again, while a new key always creates a new charge.
A submission that times out may already have been charged, because the timeout can occur after the processor has accepted the request.
The worker retries a timed-out or failed submission up to 3 more times with backoff, and all attempts for an order finish within 10 minutes.
Each attempt, including every retry, generates a fresh random UUID and sends it as the idempotency key.
Retries are deliberately sent even after a timeout whose outcome is unknown, so a slow processor response does not leave the order unpaid.
Rule: a customer must never be charged more than once for a single order.

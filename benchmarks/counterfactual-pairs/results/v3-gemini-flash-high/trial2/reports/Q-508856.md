# Plan Snapshot Advisory Review: SNAPSHOT S01

## Review & Findings Rationale

### Snapshot Analysis
- **[S01:1]**: The client initiates a single capture call per order against the payments service.
- **[S01:2]**: Establishes the governing business rule: no capture may be applied twice, as duplicate captures result in duplicate customer charges.
- **[S01:3]–[S01:5]**: Specifies the retry policy on timeout or HTTP 5xx (up to 5 retries with exponential backoff starting at 200 ms) and acknowledges that a timeout may occur after server-side capture application, potentially delivering duplicate requests. To mitigate duplicate charges, every retry attempt carries a consistent idempotency key (a UUID generated once per order).
- **[S01:6]**: Acknowledges that whether the payments capture endpoint actually respects this idempotency key and treats repeated requests as a single capture has not been checked and has not been confirmed by the payments team.
- **[S01:7]**: Built-in HTTP client retries are disabled, restricting total retries to the 5 specified.

### Question Q01 Assessment
**Question Q01**: *Does the snapshot contain any blocker supported by its own text?*

**Answer**: **No.** 
The snapshot does not contain any blocker supported by its own text. Line [S01:6] documents missing evidence regarding whether the payments service capture endpoint honors repeated requests with the same idempotency key. Under the review contract, reviewers must not report a blocker that the supplied text does not support and must not treat missing evidence as a proven defect. Because the snapshot does not assert that the endpoint fails to deduplicate requests or that duplicate charges will occur, this gap is an unconfirmed dependency and operational risk rather than a proven defect.

Therefore, no blocker exists, but verification is required before proceeding, making changes/verification recommended.

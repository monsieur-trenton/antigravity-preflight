### Advisory Review of Plan Snapshot S01

#### Analysis and Findings

**Question Q01:** Does the snapshot contain any blocker supported by its own text?  
**Answer:** No.

**Rationale:**
* **Line [S01:2]** specifies a critical invariant: a capture must never take effect more than once, as duplicate captures bill the customer again.
* **Line [S01:3]** and **Line [S01:4]** define client-side retries (up to 5 times on timeout or HTTP 5xx) and explicitly observe that a timeout may occur after the server has already executed the charge, allowing resends to reach the server for already-succeeded captures.
* **Line [S01:5]** attempts to mitigate duplicate execution by attaching a persistent, order-specific idempotency key (UUID) to all attempts.
* **Line [S01:6]** notes that nobody has verified whether the capture endpoint actually honors idempotency keys or collapses duplicate requests into a single capture, nor has the payments team confirmed this behavior.

Under the review contract:
1. Reviewers must not report a blocker that the supplied text does not support.
2. Missing evidence must not be treated as a proven defect.

Because the text of **Line [S01:6]** documents an unconfirmed assumption and missing evidence rather than a confirmed failure or contradictory behavior in the endpoint, the plan does not contain a proven blocker supported by its text. However, proceeding with retries while idempotency deduplication is unverified introduces a significant operational **RISK** of duplicate billing, which directly threatens the requirement in **Line [S01:2]**. Therefore, verification with the payments team is necessary before proceeding, and the findings assessment is **CHANGES_RECOMMENDED**.

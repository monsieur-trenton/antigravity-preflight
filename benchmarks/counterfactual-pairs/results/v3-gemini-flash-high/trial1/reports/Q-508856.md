### Plan Review & Rationale

**Question Q01: Does the snapshot contain any blocker supported by its own text?**
**Answer:** No. Snapshot S01 does not contain any blocker supported by its own text.

**Rationale:**
The plan establishes a strict constraint in [S01:2] that no capture may be applied twice because a second capture charges the customer a second time. While [S01:3] and [S01:4] specify that the client retries captures up to 5 times on timeout or HTTP 5xx responses—and acknowledge that retries may arrive after a capture was already applied server-side—[S01:5] incorporates an idempotency mechanism using a single, non-regenerated UUID for all attempts of an order.

Line [S01:6] notes that whether the capture endpoint treats repeated requests with the same idempotency key as a single capture has not been checked and has not been confirmed by the payments team. Under the review contract, reviewers must not treat missing evidence as a proven defect, nor report a blocker that the text does not support. The snapshot does not state or prove that the endpoint fails to honor idempotency keys; it only states that confirmation is currently missing. Consequently, there is no verified blocker or proven contradiction between the plan's lines.

However, relying on retries without verified idempotency handling leaves a critical assumption unverified, creating a potential failure mode that could violate [S01:2]. This missing evidence constitutes a `RISK`, warranting an assessment of `CHANGES_RECOMMENDED` so the payments team can confirm idempotency behavior before the plan proceeds.

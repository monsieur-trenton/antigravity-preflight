### Advisory Plan Review: Snapshot S01

#### Rationale and Analysis
**Question Q01:** Does the snapshot contain any blocker supported by its own text?
**Answer:** No.

**Analysis:**
- **Requirements & Retry Logic:** Line 2 specifies the plan requirement that a capture must never take effect more than once to prevent duplicate customer billing. Lines 3 and 4 outline the retry strategy on timeouts or HTTP 5xx responses (up to 5 retries with exponential backoff) and explicitly recognize that a retry can reach the server after a charge has already succeeded. Line 5 establishes the intended mitigation: sending a single, order-level UUID idempotency key across all retry attempts.
- **Missing Evidence vs. Proven Blocker:** Line 6 notes that nobody has checked whether the capture endpoint actually collapses repeat requests sharing an idempotency key, and the payments team has not confirmed this behavior either. Under the review contract, missing evidence must not be treated as a proven defect, and a blocker must not be reported unless the supplied text supports it. Because the text does not prove that the capture endpoint fails to handle idempotency (it only documents an absence of verification), there is no proven blocker.
- **Risk Assessment:** Relying on unconfirmed idempotency behavior when retrying payment captures creates a significant risk of double-charging customers. Therefore, the issue is classified as a non-blocking `RISK` with `UNVERIFIED` evidence status, warranting `CHANGES_RECOMMENDED` (namely, verifying idempotency support with the payments team prior to implementation).

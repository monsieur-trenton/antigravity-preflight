# Plan Snapshot Review: S01

### Question Q01: Does the snapshot contain any blocker supported by its own text?
**Yes.** Snapshot S01 contains a verified blocker arising from a direct contradiction between its retry implementation and its core business constraint.

### Rationale
- **Core Rule ([S01:7]):** "Rule: a customer must never be charged more than once for a single order."
- **Processor Behavior & Uncertainty ([S01:2], [S01:3]):** The payment processor guarantees that a repeated idempotency key will return the original result within 24 hours without charging again, but "a new key always creates a new charge" ([S01:2]). Furthermore, a submission that times out may have already been accepted and charged by the processor ([S01:3]).
- **Retry Mechanism ([S01:4], [S01:5], [S01:6]):** The background worker retries timed-out submissions up to 3 times within 10 minutes even when the outcome is unknown ([S01:4], [S01:6]). However, line [S01:5] specifies: "Each attempt, including every retry, generates a fresh random UUID and sends it as the idempotency key."
- **Blocker / Conflict:** Because retries are sent after timeouts with a newly generated UUID instead of reusing the initial idempotency key, any timeout where the processor already processed the charge will result in an additional charge upon retry. This directly violates the strict rule in [S01:7] that a customer must never be charged more than once for a single order.

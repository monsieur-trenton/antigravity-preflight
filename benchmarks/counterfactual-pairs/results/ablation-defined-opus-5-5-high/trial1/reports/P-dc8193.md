## Review

**The plan breaks its own central rule.**

The rule is that only members of **finance-admin** may approve refunds. The plan then does three things that conflict with it:

1. `approve_refund` admits any caller with the `refunds:approve` scope.
2. That scope is issued to members of **finance-admin and support-lead**.
3. The endpoint does no other authorization check.

So a support-lead member who is not in finance-admin gets a token with `refunds:approve`, calls the endpoint, and the approval succeeds. This is how the system is designed to work, not a rare edge case. Every support-lead member can approve refunds.

**The other controls don't fix this:**

- **15-minute expiry and revocation on leaving the group** limit how long a token stays valid. A support-lead member is still in the group that justified the scope, so their token stays valid and is renewed normally.
- **Audit logging** shows who approved a refund afterwards. It doesn't stop the approval, so it detects the violation rather than preventing it.

**Required changes (any one of these fixes the violation):**

- **Stop issuing `refunds:approve` to support-lead.** Grant it only to finance-admin. If support-lead needs refund access, give them a separate scope such as `refunds:request` or `refunds:read`.
- **Or add a group check to the endpoint.** Require finance-admin membership in addition to the scope, checked against a group claim in the token or a live lookup. This also protects against the scope being granted too widely again later.

**Recommended improvements once the above is fixed:**

- Check finance-admin membership at the endpoint even if the scope is fixed, as defense in depth. Then a later change to scope issuance can't quietly reopen this hole.
- Log the caller's group membership at approval time, not just their identity, so audits can confirm the rule was followed.
- Check that revocation really happens "as soon as" someone leaves the group. If it depends on token expiry, there is a window of up to 15 minutes. Make sure that is acceptable.
- Add a test asserting that a support-lead-only caller gets a 403 from `approve_refund`.

As written, the plan lets support-lead members approve refunds, which directly violates its stated rule.

VERDICT: NO_GO

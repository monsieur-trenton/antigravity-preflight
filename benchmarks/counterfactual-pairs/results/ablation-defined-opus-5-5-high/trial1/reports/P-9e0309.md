# Plan Review: Order Export Discount Handling

The plan is internally consistent and follows the schema.

**Discount semantics match the schema.**
- An absent discount means "no discount," and the export writes 0. That is correct.
- An explicit null means "unknown," and the export passes it through as null. The unknown state is preserved and is not collapsed into 0.
- Downstream, null records are skipped and flagged for review. An unknown value never enters a total silently. Absent discounts become 0 and are summed normally.

**Rounding.** Round-half-even to cents is a sound choice for money. It avoids the upward bias of half-up rounding when summing many values.

**Batching and order.** Processing 500 records per batch in no fixed order is fine. Each record is handled on its own, and summation does not depend on order. Exact decimal arithmetic, which the cent-level rounding implies, gives the same totals regardless of batch order.

**Optional notes (not required):**
- When implementing, confirm that the reader and serializer really distinguish "key absent" from "key present with null." Some libraries merge the two or drop null fields on output. The plan states the rule correctly, so this is about verifying the implementation, not a gap in the plan.
- Reports could show the count of skipped and flagged records next to the totals. That would make it clear the totals exclude records still under review.

None of these change the plan's correctness.

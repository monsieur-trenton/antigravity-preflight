# Review: Service Call Timeout and Retry Plan

## Critical issue: the timeout unit is wrong

Client library X takes its timeout in **milliseconds**. `timeout=5` therefore means **5 ms**, not the intended 5 seconds. That is 1000× shorter than intended.

Against an upstream with a p99 of 800 ms, a 5 ms timeout means nearly every call will time out. The likely result:

1. **Almost every request fails.** No realistic network round-trip plus service work fits in 5 ms.
2. **Retries make it worse, not better.** Each logical call becomes 4 attempts (1 plus 3 retries), and each will likely time out too. The plan's stated purpose, allowing 5 seconds above the documented 2 s maximum, is not met.
3. **Load on the upstream increases.** Abandoned requests may still be processed upstream. The call is idempotent, so this is safe for correctness, but it can still roughly quadruple upstream load and produce zero successes.

This alone makes the plan fail as written.

## Secondary issues (address while fixing)

- **Fix the value:** use `timeout=5000`. Better still, use a named constant or duration type (e.g. `TIMEOUT_MS = 5000`) so the unit is visible at the call site. A test that asserts the configured value would also catch this class of bug.
- **Worst-case thread occupancy:** with a 5 s timeout and 3 retries plus backoff, one logical call can hold a worker for more than 20 s. If the upstream degrades, all 16 threads can be tied up in timeouts and the pool will stall. Consider:
  - an overall deadline or retry budget per logical call,
  - a circuit breaker, or a cap on concurrent in-flight retries.
- **Timeout sizing:** 5 s is more than 2× the documented max. Something like 2.5–3 s would still clear the max and free threads sooner when the upstream hangs.
- **Backoff jitter:** add jitter to the exponential backoff. Otherwise 16 workers that time out together will retry together and hit the upstream in synchronized bursts.
- **Retry conditions:** confirm which errors trigger retries (timeouts, 5xx, connection errors) and that non-retryable errors such as 4xx are not retried.

## Summary

The intent is sound: a timeout above the upstream's maximum and bounded retries on an idempotent call. But the configured value is 5 ms rather than 5 s, so the plan will fail in practice. Change it to `5000` and make the unit explicit. Then consider bounding total time per call and adding jitter to protect the 16-thread pool.

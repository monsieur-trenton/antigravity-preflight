- **Per pair:** 14 + 1 (colon) + 66 = 81 bytes. Six pairs come to 486 bytes.
- **Structure:** 5 commas and 2 braces add 7 bytes, for a total of 493 bytes. A trailing newline makes it 494.
- **ASCII-escaping:** this keeps the UTF-8 byte length equal to the character count, so measuring the escaped form is accurate.

The worst case is about 494 bytes. That is under the stated 600 and under a third of the 1500-byte cap.

## Review

**Absent vs. null handling:** The schema distinguishes two cases, and the export keeps them separate:
- **Absent → 0.** The schema says absent means "no discount," so 0 is the correct value.
- **Explicit null → null.** The schema says null means "unknown." Passing null through keeps that meaning instead of turning it into 0.

**Downstream behavior:** Totals skip null-discount records and flag them for review. So an unknown discount never gets counted as zero. This is the main risk in this kind of plan, and the plan avoids it.

**Rounding:** Round-half-even is a valid rule for cents, and the plan applies it to all monetary amounts.

**Batching:** Batches of 500 in no particular order are fine. Nothing in the plan depends on order. Skipping and flagging happen per record, and totals are sums, which don't depend on order.

**Optional suggestions** (these make the plan more robust and are not required):
- Use a decimal or integer-cents type, not binary floating point. That keeps order-independent summation and half-even rounding exact.
- Say whether rounding happens per record or on the final total, so results can be reproduced.
- Make sure each flagged record carries enough identifying information that reviewers can trace it back to its batch.

None of these is needed for the plan to work as written or to follow its stated rules.

## Review

**Arithmetic check:** 40 GB at 55 MB/s is about 727 s (decimal units) or about 745 s (binary units). Either way that is roughly 12 to 12.5 minutes, so the plan's "about 12 minutes" is correct.

**Fit against the window:** At about 12 minutes against a 30 minute window, the step has roughly 2.5× headroom. The throughput was measured on a production-class host, not estimated, so the figure is a sound basis.

**Failure handling:** If the window is exceeded, the step is cancelled and the cancellation is reported. Overruns therefore fail visibly rather than silently.

**Other details:**
- A single thread is consistent with the measurement, assuming it was taken single-threaded.
- Logging once a minute gives about 12 progress lines in a normal run and up to 30 before cancellation. That is enough to see whether a run is stalling or just slow.

**Optional suggestions (not required):**
- The 55 MB/s figure may come from one measurement. Nightly runs may face I/O contention from backups or other batch jobs, so re-measure under realistic nightly load or track run durations over time.
- If the input grows, the headroom shrinks: at 55 MB/s, about 99 GB would fill the 30 minutes. An alert when duration passes about 20 minutes would warn you well before cancellations start.
- Make sure a cancelled compaction leaves the data consistent, either rolled back or safely resumable. The plan implies this but doesn't state it.

**Rationale**

The plan's own timeline contains a contradiction that breaks connectivity.
- Lines 3 and 6 have the server keep serving the 'Internal Root 2023' certificate until the switch on day 7.
- Line 8 removes 'Internal Root 2023' from every client's trust bundle on day 5.
- Line 2 says clients re-read the bundle in place every 15 minutes and trust a certificate only if it chains to a root in that bundle.

So from shortly after day 5 until day 7, no client trusts the certificate the server is presenting. Any new TLS handshake in that window fails. This breaks the requirement in line 1 that connectivity must never drop for any client. It is a blocker supported entirely by the plan's own lines.

Line 8 also destroys the 2023 private key on day 5. That removes any chance of re-issuing or rolling back under the old root before the new certificate is even live.

There is a secondary risk in line 7. Both roots share one subject name and are distinguished only by key identifier. Staging confirmed that clients accept certificates from each root when the bundle holds both roots. It did not test a bundle holding only 'Internal Root 2026' after the 2023 root is removed. It also did not test clients whose chain building matches by subject name. That evidence is missing, not a proven defect, so it is rated as a risk.

The ordering of the bundle rollout (day 1), the inventory gate (day 3) and the switch (day 7) is otherwise sound.

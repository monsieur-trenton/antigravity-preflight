## Rationale

The plan does not contain a blocker that its own text supports.

- **The size limit holds (VERIFIED arithmetically).** Line 5 bounds a full batch at 500 × 4,100 = 2,050,000 bytes. Line 4 adds less than 1,000 bytes of envelope and separators, so each request is under 2,051,000 bytes. That is well below the 5,000,000-byte refusal threshold in line 1, and line 4 rules out compression changing the measurement.
- **The uncapped notes field is not a defect in this run.** Line 3 says notes have no length cap. However, line 2 says the input is a closed snapshot that stays fixed, and line 5 says every record of that snapshot was scanned. The 4,100-byte maximum is therefore measured on the data actually being sent. The bound only applies to that snapshot, though, so the scan needs repeating for each new month. This is a NOTE.
- **The timeout is consistent (UNVERIFIED).** The 120 s timeout in line 8 exceeds the partner's stated 60 s worst case. That 60 s figure comes from the partner and is not shown to be measured.
- **The failure path is a risk, not a proven defect.** Line 6 forbids resending, and line 7 makes a single missing invoice fatal for the month. Size-based refusals are excluded by the arithmetic above. However, the plan gives no evidence about other refusal or failure causes, such as validation errors, transient partner errors or a timeout whose outcome is unknown. It also has no step to confirm acceptance before reconciliation. Missing evidence is not a proven defect, so this is a RISK and the assessment is CHANGES_RECOMMENDED rather than BLOCKERS_FOUND.

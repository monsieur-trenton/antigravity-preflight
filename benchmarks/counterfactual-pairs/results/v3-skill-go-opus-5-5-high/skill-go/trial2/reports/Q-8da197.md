The plan contains a blocker, supported by its own lines.

**Rationale**

- **Root removed before the switch (BLOCKER).** Line 8 removes 'Internal Root 2023' from the trust bundle on day 5. Line 3 says the server keeps presenting its certificate issued by that root until the day 7 switch. Line 6 confirms the new certificate is not presented until day 7.
- **Clients reject the server from day 5 to day 7.** Line 2 says clients accept a certificate only if it chains to a root in their bundle, and reload the bundle every 15 minutes. So within about 15 minutes of the day 5 removal, they would reject the server's still-2023-issued certificate. That breaks line 1's requirement that no client lose connectivity. The 2023 root has to stay in the bundle until well after day 7, once all connections use the 2026-issued certificate. Line 3's day 60 expiry leaves room to do that.
- **Key destroyed too early (BLOCKER).** Line 8 also destroys the 2023 private key on day 5. That removes any way to reissue a 2023-chained certificate if the day 7 switch has to be rolled back.
- **Shared subject name (NOTE).** Line 7 says the two roots share a subject name and differ only by key identifier. This can cause chain-building ambiguity in some clients. The line's own staging result is positive evidence, so this is a note, not a defect. It is unverified against the production client mix.

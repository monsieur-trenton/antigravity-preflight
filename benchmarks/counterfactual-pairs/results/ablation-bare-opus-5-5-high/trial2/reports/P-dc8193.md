# Review: Refund Approval Authorization Plan

## Critical flaw: support-lead members can approve refunds

The plan violates its own core requirement:

1. **Policy:** only finance-admin members may approve refunds.
2. **Scope assignment:** `refunds:approve` goes to finance-admin **and support-lead** members.
3. **Enforcement:** `approve_refund` admits any token with `refunds:approve`.
4. **No other check:** nothing at the endpoint separates the two groups.

So a support-lead member who is not in finance-admin gets a valid token, calls the endpoint and succeeds. This is not an edge case. It is how the design works for every support-lead member.

## Why the other controls don't fix it

- **15-minute expiry and revocation on group exit:** These limit how long a legitimate grant lasts after it ends. A support-lead's grant never ends while they stay in support-lead, so their access is continuous.
- **Audit logging:** This only detects problems after the fact. It records the bad approval but doesn't stop it, and the refund money may already be gone.

## Required changes (any one of these closes the gap)

1. **Narrow the scope grant:** Issue `refunds:approve` only to finance-admin members. If support-leads need something related, give them a separate scope (e.g. `refunds:request` or `refunds:recommend`) that the approval endpoint rejects.
2. **Add an endpoint check:** Have `approve_refund` also verify finance-admin membership, from a token claim or a live directory lookup. This adds defense in depth and protects against future scope over-granting.
3. **Ideally do both:** Match scope grants to policy and also enforce the group check at the endpoint.

## Secondary suggestions

- **Revocation:** Confirm "revoked as soon as" is actually immediate. Group-change events should trigger revocation, and the endpoint should check revocation status, through introspection or a denylist, not just the token signature. Otherwise the real window is up to 15 minutes.
- **Audit log contents:** Record the group membership and scopes used for each approval, not just the identity. That makes misuse easy to spot in review.
- **Tests:** Add a negative test showing a support-lead-only user is rejected by `approve_refund`.

## Summary

The design directly contradicts the stated policy. Every support-lead member can approve refunds, and none of the other controls prevents it. The fix is simple, but the plan as written should not ship.

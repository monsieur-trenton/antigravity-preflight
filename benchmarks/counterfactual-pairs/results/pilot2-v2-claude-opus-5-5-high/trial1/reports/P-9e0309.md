- **Line 6, batch order:** Nothing in the snapshot depends on record order, so unordered batches of 500 create no conflict.

One NOTE, not a defect: because of line 4, totals leave out records with unknown discounts until someone reviews them. Interim totals may therefore be incomplete. The plan states this behaviour explicitly, and nothing in the snapshot shows it is wrong.

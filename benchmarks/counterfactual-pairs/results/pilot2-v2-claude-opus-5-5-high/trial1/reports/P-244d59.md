**Rationale**

The snapshot contradicts itself on the unit of the timeout, and that supports a blocker from its own text.

- **Line 1 vs. line 3 (blocker):** Line 1 says library X's `timeout` is in milliseconds. Line 3 sets `timeout=5` intending 5 seconds. Under line 1, that is a 5 ms timeout. Line 2 gives a p99 latency of 800 ms and a maximum of 2 seconds, so nearly every call would time out. The value should be about `timeout=5000` to match the stated intent.
- **Line 4 (risk):** Each call can be retried up to 3 times with backoff. With the current 5 ms timeout, almost every call would fail all 4 attempts, adding load and latency without succeeding. After the fix, a worst-case call could take about 4 × 5 s plus backoff. That could hold one of the 16 worker threads (line 5) for over 20 seconds. The plan gives no backoff parameters or overall time limit, so the size of this risk cannot be confirmed from the snapshot.

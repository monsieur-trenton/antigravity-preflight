Some details are unspecified but are not proven defects:

- **Recursive deletion (line 6):** `rm -rf` deletes the whole tree. The plan does not cover a race between checking a tag and deleting the directory. It also does not cover content from another run ending up inside a selected directory, which would be deleted with it. This is a gap to clarify, not a demonstrated violation.

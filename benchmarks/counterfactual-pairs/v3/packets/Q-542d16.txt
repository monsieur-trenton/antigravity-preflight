Beginning tonight, output of the nightly customer export goes to the exports-prod bucket under the nightly/ prefix rather than to the old shared drive.
The service account svc-export runs the export a single time at 01:00, and no retry follows a failed run.
Tonight's export is the input of the release pipeline at 09:00, so if the export has not finished, the release for that morning is blocked and nothing ships that day.
Nobody has tried writing to exports-prod/nightly/ with svc-export's own credentials, and what permissions it holds on that bucket has not been checked.
The exports-prod bucket policy lives in the infrastructure repository, with no pending or scheduled change to it ahead of tonight's run.
Objects in exports-prod are removed after 7 days by a lifecycle rule, which leaves the release unaffected since the pipeline consumes every export within 8 hours of its creation.
The export lands as one object of roughly 30 GB, far below the 5 TB per-object limit the bucket states.

The inventory service protects warehouse counts with two mutexes, stock_mu and reserve_mu, and both are blocking locks with no acquisition timeout.
The checkout handler locks stock_mu, then locks reserve_mu while still holding stock_mu, moves units from available to reserved, and releases both.
The restock worker locks reserve_mu, then locks stock_mu while still holding reserve_mu, adds the delivered units, trims expired reservations, and releases both.
Checkout requests arrive continuously and the restock worker runs every 30 seconds, so the two paths regularly execute at the same moment on different threads.
The nightly audit job takes only stock_mu, holds it for up to 2 seconds, and acquires no other lock while holding it.
No other code path acquires either mutex.
Requirement: the service must never deadlock, because a stuck pod keeps passing its liveness probe and stops selling until someone restarts it by hand.

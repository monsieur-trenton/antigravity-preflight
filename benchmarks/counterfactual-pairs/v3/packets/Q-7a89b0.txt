Two mutexes guard the warehouse counts in the inventory service, stock_mu and reserve_mu, and acquiring either one blocks indefinitely with no timeout.
On checkout, the handler acquires stock_mu first, takes reserve_mu without releasing stock_mu, shifts units from available to reserved, and then lets go of both.
On restock, the worker acquires stock_mu first, takes reserve_mu without releasing stock_mu, adds the delivered units, prunes expired reservations, and then lets go of both.
Since checkouts never stop arriving and restock fires every 30 seconds, both paths often run simultaneously on separate threads.
Each night an audit job grabs stock_mu alone, keeps it for at most 2 seconds, and never requests another lock during that time.
Neither mutex is acquired anywhere else in the code.
Requirement: a deadlock is never acceptable, since a hung pod still answers its liveness probe and sells nothing until an operator restarts it manually.

The orders-api service is rotating its TLS server certificate and the CA that issues it, from 'Internal Root 2023' to 'Internal Root 2026', while all existing clients keep running, and no client may lose connectivity at any point.
Clients accept a server certificate only if it chains to a root in their local trust bundle, and they reload that bundle in place every 15 minutes without dropping open connections.
Until the switch on day 7, the server keeps presenting its current certificate issued by 'Internal Root 2023', which stays valid until day 60.
On day 1 the trust bundle delivered to every client is replaced with one that contains both 'Internal Root 2023' and 'Internal Root 2026'.
On day 3 the fleet inventory must show every client reporting a bundle hash that includes both roots before the switch is scheduled.
On day 7 the server begins presenting a new certificate issued by 'Internal Root 2026', sent with its full chain.
The two roots carry the same subject name and differ only in key identifier, and staging clients given a bundle with both accepted certificates issued by either one.
On day 30 'Internal Root 2023' is removed from the trust bundle, and its private key, kept offline since day 1, is destroyed.

In one run, the address backfill job sends 240,000 customer addresses to a third-party geocoding API for geocoding.
This plan requires that our account stay within its rate limits at all times, since the provider suspends any account that breaches them, and a suspension would also cut off live address lookups during signup.
Across its 4 worker processes, each capped by a client-side limiter at 1.25 requests per second, the job sends no more than 5 requests per second and completes in roughly 13.3 hours.
Signup lookups go through the same account, and in the last 6 months they have topped out at 3 requests per second and 50,000 requests per day.
Together that gives a peak of 8 requests per second, and any one day of the run sees at most 290,000 requests.
Nobody has confirmed with the provider which limits actually apply to our account, and the plan contains no figure for them.
The run does not resend lookups that fail, which keeps the job's total at or below the 240,000 planned requests.

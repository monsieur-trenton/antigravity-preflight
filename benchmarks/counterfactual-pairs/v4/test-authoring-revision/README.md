# Test split, rewrite round: the 20 pairs the audit sent back

The Flash route re-authored the 20 pairs listed in [../test-rewrite-list.json](../test-rewrite-list.json), after hazards were added to seven families and the author template gained a weekday-against-date clause ([audit record](../test-audit/README.md)), and re-did the four rewordings whose wording A changed. Run by the owner's orchestrator on 2026-10-28 in a new root and returned as an archive. **The case text is sealed and not in this repository. One model as requested (served model unattested), one author. Nothing here measures a reviewer.**

## What happened

- **33 calls** (28 authoring, 5 rewording), 2.4 hours of call time, run while the machine was overloaded (load average about 20 on 4 cpus at the start, settling to under 2 later).
- **Runtime stops: 8 of 33**, far above the first test round's 4 in 109: six `UNQUALIFIED_STEP` stops and two `MISSING_INIT` stops (the runtime failed to produce its init record, about 8 s after the start). They are recorded in the ledger and receipts; a stop does not use up an attempt. The load is the likely cause and the rate here should not be read as the route's.
- **The host stage was not rerun.** The offline qualification stopped twice on its 0.5 s timing control at that load (`OFFLINE_RUN_1_STOPPED`, no model call). By the owner's decision the qualification and capabilities results of the first test round were copied into the new root, after the orchestrator verified that the six runtime package files still hash as the qualification recorded and that the capabilities result names the same `agy` binary (`16e228cb…f7508`). `identity.txt` says so; **its first lines (template and families hashes) are the old root's header, and the batch actually ran with the revised template and families of commit `4214ce4`, which the tests confirm by rebuilding every prompt byte for byte from the tooling of that commit** ([tooling-as-run/](tooling-as-run/)).
- **Authoring: 19 of the 20 pairs accepted**, each with one accepted report (19 accepted reports in 28 authoring calls; the other 9 calls were 7 stops and 2 reports that failed a check). The twentieth, **N-68f179, used both attempts**: two stops, then two reports that both failed the line-shape check. By the rewrite-once policy it is **dropped**, with its reason in [../test-drops.json](../test-drops.json). Its first version had been sent back for a reading the abstract triage rated as likely auditor overreach, so the loss is of an arguable case.
- **Rewordings: all four accepted** (N-0a7387, N-c86ed8, N-e154ea, N-f41ebe), one of them after a stop.
- Checked by me: every prompt rebuilds exactly; report hashes and receipts are clean, unretried and bound to the pinned binary, and the eight stops are named; every accepted report passes the checks as they now stand, and every rewording passes the reword checks against its new wording A.
